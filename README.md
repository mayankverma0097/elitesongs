# 🎵 EliteSongs

## Discover Music with EliteSongs

EliteSongs is a music and playlist experience created for people who love discovering great songs. Explore curated playlists, find memorable tracks, and press play whenever you're ready to listen.

Whether you're looking for songs to play in the background, music for a late night, or something new to add to your playlist, EliteSongs makes discovering music simple and enjoyable.
